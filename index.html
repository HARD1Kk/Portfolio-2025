I'm Hardik, an AI/ML Engineer based in Delhi and a recent B.Tech CSE graduate (MAIT Delhi). My work centers on retrieval-augmented generation: turning messy real-world documents into structured, queryable knowledge and serving it through answers you can actually trust.
